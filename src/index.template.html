    Payment request decoder

    Paste any BOLT11 invoice (lnbc…, lntb…, lnbcrt…) or BOLT12 offer (lno1…). The decoder is local: bech32 checksum, 35-bit timestamp, tagged fields.

    A BOLT12 offer has no payment_hash, so preimage matching only holds with a BOLT11 invoice. BOLT12 is decoded here as the Lexe offer shape.
